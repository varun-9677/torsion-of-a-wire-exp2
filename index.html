🎯 Aim & Conclusion

Aim: To study how a wire twists under a torque and to find the rigidity modulus of its material.

Conclusion: The twist angle is directly proportional to the torque and to the length of the wire. It is inversely proportional to the fourth power of the radius (r⁴) and to the rigidity modulus η.
